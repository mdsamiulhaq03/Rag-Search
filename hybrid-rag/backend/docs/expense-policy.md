# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## EXP-204: Travel Expenses
Policy EXP-204 covers business travel. Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours;
premium economy is allowed for longer flights. Hotels are capped at USD 180 per night in standard
cities and USD 260 per night in high-cost cities (see appendix B). Daily meal allowance is USD 60.

## EXP-310: Equipment Purchases
Policy EXP-310 covers home-office equipment. Each employee may claim up to USD 500 every two years
for a chair, desk, monitor, or headset. Laptops are issued by IT and are not reimbursable.

## Submitting a Claim
Submit receipts in the Finance app within 30 days of the expense. Claims are approved by your
direct manager and paid in the next payroll cycle. Missing receipts require a signed declaration.
