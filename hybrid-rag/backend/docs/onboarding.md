# New Joiner Onboarding

## First Day
On your first day, collect your laptop and badge from reception at 9:00. Your manager will walk you
through the team's goals and introduce your onboarding buddy.

## First Week
Complete the mandatory trainings in the Learning portal: Security Awareness, Code of Conduct,
and Data Privacy. Set up MFA on all accounts (see IT Security Guidelines).

## Probation
The probation period is 3 months. A review meeting with your manager happens at the end of month 2
and at the end of month 3.

## Useful Contacts
HR questions: hr@company.example. Payroll questions: payroll@company.example.
