Acme Insight - Product FAQ

What is Acme Insight?
Acme Insight is our analytics dashboard for retail clients. It shows daily sales, inventory levels,
and demand forecasts.

Which plans are available?
Starter (up to 5 users), Growth (up to 50 users), and Enterprise (unlimited users, SSO, and a 99.9% uptime SLA).

How often is data refreshed?
Sales data refreshes every 15 minutes. Forecasts are recalculated nightly at 02:00 UTC.

Error code AI-503 means the data connector timed out. Ask the client to re-authorize the connector
in Settings > Integrations. If it persists for more than one hour, escalate to tier-2 support.
