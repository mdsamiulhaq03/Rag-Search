# HR Leave Policy (HR-101)

## Annual Leave
Full-time employees accrue 20 days of annual leave per calendar year, credited monthly at 1.67 days.
Up to 5 unused days may be carried over into the next year; anything beyond that expires on March 31.
Leave requests must be submitted in the HR portal at least 10 working days in advance.

## Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. Absences longer than 2 consecutive days
require a medical certificate uploaded to the HR portal within 5 days of returning.

## Parental Leave
Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks.
Parental leave can be taken in up to two blocks within 12 months of the birth or adoption.

## Public Holidays
The company observes all national public holidays. The holiday calendar is published every December.
