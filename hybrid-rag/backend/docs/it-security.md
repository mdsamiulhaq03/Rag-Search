# IT Security Guidelines

## Passwords and MFA
Passwords must be at least 14 characters. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for email,
VPN, and all cloud tools. Use the company password manager; never reuse passwords across services.

## Lost or Stolen Devices
Report a lost or stolen laptop or phone to the IT helpdesk immediately at extension 4357 (HELP)
or via the #it-help channel. IT will remotely wipe the device. Do not wait until the next working day.

## Phishing
Forward suspicious emails to phishing@company.example using the "Report Phish" button.
Never enter your credentials on a page reached through an email link.

## VPN
The VPN is required when accessing internal systems from outside the office network.
Install the VPN client from the Self-Service portal.
